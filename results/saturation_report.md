# Logit saturation of the trained policies

Generated by `scripts/saturation_report.py` (756 policy files). Policy: z = L·tanh(Wx/L), L = logit_clip. The chain-rule factor of every score-function gradient is sech²(Wx/L): 0.42 at |Wx|/L = 1, 0.027 at 2.5, 1.8e-4 at 5, and exactly 0 in float64 beyond about 19. Bounded probabilities therefore do not imply steerable weights (tests/test_saturation.py: W = [[300], [-300]], x = [1], L = 3 gives probabilities [0.9975, 0.0025] and an identically zero gradient for both actions).

For each policy: u = |Wx|/L over the legal (action, state) pairs of the states listed. 'served' pools the iterate-mix snapshots; 'W' is the final iterate. Saturated = u > 2.5. 'min p' is the smallest served (ε-free) probability of a legal action at those states.

## Reading

- **u > 2.5 is attenuation, not death.** sech² = 0.027 at u = 2.5, 0.0067 at u = 3.19 (the largest committed value), and < 1e-3 only beyond u ≈ 4.1. Every committed policy stays below u = 3.2 and has no legal pair with sech² < 1e-3. The CRMArena and SSR policies, and the sim v1 policies, never reach u = 2.5.
- **Committed `results/mas/policy_awr_orm.json`** (AWR, 300 full-batch epochs at lr 2, fixed λ = 2) has 2.6% of its legal (action, decision) pairs above 2.5, all on the low side: actions pushed toward the floor, where the gradient through the clip is damped 37× to 150×. It is a one-shot policy that is never warm-started, so this affects only how far further training could move it.
- **The sim policy_v2 files** (warm-started from policy_v1 and retrained on v2) have about 2% of pairs above 2.5, all on the high side (a preferred action's logit pinned near +L). There, the attenuation slows moving mass away from the incumbent's favourite action, which is exactly what a drift update must do.
- **The continual AWR arm is the one place where the weights are effectively frozen.** It warm-starts every day from the previous day's policy (`train_awr_day(..., init=prev)`), so |Wx| accumulates: by day 40, u reaches about 13, close to half of the legal pairs are above 2.5, and up to 61% have sech² < 1e-3. Almost all of these are on the low side, so those actions cannot be brought back by any later reward signal through this parameterisation. The continual CGRPO arm and the oracles stay at u ≤ 4.2, with sech² < 1e-3 in at most 0.7% of pairs (one policy). Comparisons of AWR against CGRPO in the continual runs therefore conflate the update rule with the AWR arm's loss of plasticity in the soft clip.
- MAS-continual states are a uniform-behaviour pass, not the logged days (those logs are not saved). A uniform pass covers more of the state space than the served policy does, so the fractions here are indicative of, not equal to, the fractions at the states the arms actually visited.
