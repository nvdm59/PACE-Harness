# MAS per-decision estimates: decision unit vs task unit

Generated by `scripts/mas_perdecision_units.py` from `results/mas/mas_b2b.jsonl`: the 30 uniform-exploration episodes (64 logged decisions; decisions per episode min 1, median 2, max 4), policy `results/mas/policy_awr_orm.json`, ORM `results/mas/quality_model.json`, 200 episode-bootstrap resamples (seed 0), 90% CIs. `results/mas/RESULTS.md` is unchanged; its section 5 rows are the decision-unit rows below.

Estimands (see `pace/experiments/mas/offpolicy.py`):

- **decision unit**: every logged decision row has weight 1. Quality is the decision-weighted mean of the one-decision-intervention value, so an episode with n decisions counts n times. Cost is the mean over rows of the cost-to-go from the intervened decision; cost-to-go values of one episode overlap, so this is not a cost per task.
- **task unit**: each episode's rows are weighted 1/n_decisions, so every task counts once. Quality is the task-average of the one-decision-intervention value. Cost is the total episode cost per task, prefix + cost-to-go, where the prefix includes the policy-independent planner + first-worker cost ($0.0214/task here, which is not in any logged step) and the logged steps before the decision.

ESS: decision unit is Kish over decision rows (out of n decisions); task unit is Kish over tasks of the per-task weight Σ_t w_t / n_decisions (out of n tasks). Under the task unit the behaviour row equals the episode-level on-policy mean, as it should: both are means over tasks.

## Reading

The unit changes the sign of the outcome-quality lift that DR reports for the AWR policy: -0.062 against the behaviour under the decision unit, +0.038 under the task unit (SNIPS: -0.004 vs -0.006). Both lifts sit well inside 90% CIs that are about 0.3 to 0.5 wide on 30 tasks, so neither unit supports a quality claim on this log. The two units differ because exploration episodes have 1 to 4 decisions, and the decision unit up-weights the long ones. The decision-unit cost (mean cost-to-go per decision row) is not on the scale of the budget. The task-unit cost is total cost per task: DR 0.0581 against the behaviour's 0.0758, which can be compared with a per-task budget directly. Both units still value one changed decision with the rest of the trajectory as logged; neither is the full-policy value (the episode-level row).
