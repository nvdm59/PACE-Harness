# τ-bench results (pass^1)

Agent = our tier model (litellm, spend ledger); user simulator fixed to the cheap tier (Haiku 4.5) for every arm, its cost logged separately and excluded from agent cost. Quality = τ-bench's own `Env.calculate_reward` (0/1). Reported metric is pass^1 (one trial per task per tier); τ-bench's pass^k (k > 1) is not estimated. Behavior: uniform tier per task, CONTINUE until the episode ends, END forced at step 30 (τ-bench's max_num_steps). Generated by `python -m pace.experiments.taubench.run analyze`.

### DR gate on the realised log (one uniformly drawn tier per task)

Candidate: the constrained cgrpo-lookup policy trained on all 60 tasks (λ̄ = 0.06/USD). Exact value on the 60 tasks (all arms logged): **quality 0.687, cost $0.2620**. Uniform behavior exact: quality 0.661, cost $0.2773. Gate: eps_q = 0.03, budget $0.28 + tol $0.014.

**DECISION: REVERT**

Estimator error vs exact on this draw: quality IPS +0.144, SNIPS +0.067, DR +0.066; cost IPS +0.0163, SNIPS -0.0096, DR -0.0117.

criteria: DR-quality lower CI 0.610 >= q_prev - eps = 0.661 (incumbent on this log) - 0.03 = 0.631 [FAIL];  DR-cost upper CI 0.2829 <= budget + tol = 0.2912 [ok]
(n = 180 logged episodes over 60 tasks, max cumulative ratio 2.6)

**DECISION: REVERT**

With every arm logged at propensity 1/3, IPS, SNIPS and DR all reduce to the exact value (the importance weights over a task's three episodes sum to 3·Σπ = 3); the CI is then pure task-sampling uncertainty from 60 tasks.
